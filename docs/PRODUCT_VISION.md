# Trinetra Restaurant OS — Product Vision

Trinetra Restaurant OS is a standalone commercial SaaS product developed under Trinetra Digital Solution.

It is designed to replace traditional restaurant POS software with a modern, premium, enterprise-grade operating system for restaurants.

The product must feel:
- Fast
- Reliable
- Realtime
- Touch friendly
- Keyboard friendly
- Tablet friendly
- Mobile friendly
- Commercially valuable

The long-term vision is a flagship restaurant platform that can later support:
- Self signup
- Subscription purchase
- One-time license purchase
- Marketplace integrations
- Multi-tenant SaaS expansion

These future capabilities are not part of the current scope.

Current focus is only the operational excellence of the Restaurant OS itself.

The product must support real restaurant staff and real workflows.
Every screen must help a user complete work faster.
Every interaction should reduce operational friction.

The CRM currently provisions restaurants.
The Restaurant OS handles operations.
The two must remain logically separated.
